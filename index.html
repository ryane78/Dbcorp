How it works with us.

Eight stops from empty plot to keys. Tap any stop on the route to see what happens there.

  1. We meet on your plot. We walk the plot with you: sun, access, levels, neighbours and your society's rules for your street.
  2. We discuss your needs. Room by room, we write down how your family lives now and how you want to live in this house.
  3. We design it and render it. Your ideas become floor plans and a 3D render of your home, so you can see it before it exists and share it with family.
  4. Drawings and approvals. Architecture, structure and services are drawn in full and submitted to CDA, DHA or Bahria. Ink before concrete.
  5. One quote, one contract. Priced against the approved drawings with material grades written in. Payments are tied to stages, so you always know what you are paying for.
  6. The bones go up. Foundation, columns and slabs. A-grade steel and zero cut corners.
  7. Finishes and interiors. Glazing, stone, millwork and light, specified to the millimetre and installed to the millimetre.
  8. Welcome home. A final walk-through together, every snag closed, and the keys change hands. You live here now.

Frequently asked questions

Anything else, ask us on the free call.

Who designs and builds houses in Islamabad?

DBCorp (Design Build Corporation) is a design-build firm in Islamabad. One team handles architecture, approvals, construction and interiors, from an empty plot to handing over the keys.

What does design-build mean?

One firm both designs and builds your house under one contract. You deal with one team instead of an architect, a contractor and an interior designer separately, and nobody can blame someone else for a problem.

Do you handle approvals from CDA, DHA and Bahria Town?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and manage the submission to the authority that governs your plot.

Can you build a 10 marla or 1 kanal house?

Yes. We design and build homes from 10 marla plots up to multi-kanal estates.

How much does it cost to build a house in Islamabad?

It depends on plot size, covered area, your society's rules and the finish level you choose. Try our construction cost calculator for a quick estimate, or get a realistic range for your plot on the free 10-minute call.

What is the difference between grey structure and turnkey construction?

Grey structure is the foundation, columns, beams, slabs, brickwork, plaster and concealed pipes. Turnkey adds everything after that: flooring, doors and windows, bathrooms, kitchen, electrical fittings, paint and woodwork, so the house is ready to move into. We do both.

Which approvals do I need to build a house in Islamabad?

Your building plan (the map) must be approved before construction starts, by the authority that governs your plot: CDA for sectors such as E, F, G and I, or the society itself in DHA, Bahria Town and most private housing schemes. When the house is finished you apply for a completion certificate. We prepare the drawings and handle the submission.

I live abroad. Can you build my house?

Yes. One firm and one contract means one point of contact, and you can follow every stage of the build from wherever you are.

Is DBCorp the same as D B Corp Ltd?

No. DBCorp (Design Build Corporation) is a house design and construction firm based in Islamabad, Pakistan. It is not related to D B Corp Ltd, the Indian media company that publishes Dainik Bhaskar.

Do you work outside Islamabad?

We're based in Islamabad and also build in Rawalpindi, Lahore and Karachi.
