    How accurate is this calculator?

    It works out your covered area from the plot and floors, prices the grey structure from material quantities and current market rates, and prices finishing by level. Expect a real quote to land inside the range shown. Only a drawing-based quote gives an exact number.

    What is the difference between grey structure and complete?

    Grey structure is the foundation, columns, beams, slabs, brickwork, plaster and concealed pipes. Complete adds flooring, doors, windows, bathrooms, kitchen, electrical fittings, paint and woodwork.

    Why is the per square foot rate different from what contractors quote?

    Contractors quote different things under the same name. Some leave out bathrooms, kitchens or their own profit. Ask what each rate includes, and compare like for like.

    Why does a basement cost so much more?

    A basement needs deep excavation, retaining walls, heavier steel and waterproofing. Its structure costs roughly one and a half times more per square foot than the floors above.

    What does the estimate leave out?

    The boundary wall and gate, solar, air conditioning, landscaping, architecture and structural design, map approval fees and utility connections. We price these separately on the call.
